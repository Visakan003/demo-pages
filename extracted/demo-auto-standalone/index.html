Questions

Good to know before you book.

Will servicing with you affect my manufacturer warranty?
No. You're free to have your car serviced at an independent garage, provided the work follows the manufacturer's schedule and uses parts of matching quality. That's how we service every car, and we record it in your service book or the digital service record.
How much is an MOT, and what if it fails?
£45 for cars and light vans. If it fails, you get the photo report and a fixed price for each repair. If we carry out the work, the retest is free within 10 working days.
Do I need to leave the car all day?
Not always. MOTs, tyres and air-con regas take under an hour and you're welcome to wait. Services and bigger repairs usually need a morning drop-off. The booking card shows a realistic ready time for each job.
What happens if you find something else wrong?
We stop, photograph it and text you a fixed price. Nothing extra is done without your OK. If it's not urgent, we'll mark it as an advisory so you can plan for it.
Can you check a car before I buy it?
Yes. Book a pre-purchase inspection and bring the car in, or ask the seller to. It's a 150-point check with a diagnostic scan, a road test and a written report you can use to negotiate.
Do you work on hybrid and electric cars?
Yes for servicing, brakes, tyres, suspension, air-con and MOTs. High-voltage battery repairs go to a specialist partner, and we'll tell you up front if that's where your job is heading.
