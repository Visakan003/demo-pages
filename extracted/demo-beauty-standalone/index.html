Our approach

Looked after, not sold to.

We'd rather you left feeling rested than leave with a bag of products. Here's what that means in practice.

A beautician who knows you

Your notes stay with your beautician, so you don't have to explain your skin from scratch every visit.

Qualified and insured

Every beautician holds a Level 3 qualification in beauty therapy and is fully insured for the treatments they offer.

Patch tests, properly

Tints and lash lifts get a patch test at least 48 hours before. We won't skip it, even when we're busy.

Gentle, fragrance-free options

Sensitive skin? Tell us and we'll use fragrance-free products throughout. No questions, no fuss.

Honest advice

If a treatment isn't right for your skin, we'll say so. We're beauticians, not doctors, so anything that needs medical advice goes to your GP.

Quiet by design

Three treatment rooms, soft lighting and staggered bookings, so you'll rarely share the waiting area with anyone.

Questions

Before your first visit.

How do I know which treatment is right for me?
If you're unsure, book a 30-minute skin consultation. You'll leave with a clear idea of what's worth trying, and the £30 comes off your first facial. You can also call the studio and we'll help you choose.
Do I need a patch test?
For brow and lash tints and for lash lifts, yes: a quick one at least 48 hours before your appointment. You can pop in for it, or have it done at the end of another visit.
Can I change or cancel my booking?
Of course. Use the link in your confirmation email or call us. Changes are free up to 24 hours before. After that, we may charge half the treatment price, because the time was held for you.
I'm pregnant, or I have a skin condition. Can I still book?
Often, yes. Many treatments are fine and a few aren't. Let us know when you book and we'll talk it through. For skin conditions we may ask you to check with your GP first. We're beauticians rather than medical practitioners, so we won't treat anything that needs a doctor.
What should I expect from a facial?
An hour of cleansing, exfoliation, a mask and massage, tailored to how your skin is that day. Most people leave with skin that feels clean, soft and comfortable. Results vary from person to person, and we'll be honest about what a facial can and can't do.
Is there parking nearby?
There's pay-and-display parking on the surrounding streets, and buses stop a few minutes away on Raeburn Place. The entrance is step-free, and we're happy to help if you need a hand.
